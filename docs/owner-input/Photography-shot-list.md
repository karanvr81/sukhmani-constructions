# Photography to collect

Provide original, highest-quality files and permission for website use. Confirm these are Sukhmani installations; filenames alone do not establish ownership. Give a general suburb, service/stage, capture month and accurate description. Ask permission before naming clients or showing identifiable people. Avoid exact addresses, readable plates, screen credentials or recordings of private activity.

**First priority — one actual camera installation**

1. Full installed pole showing the complete supplied setup.
2. Solar panel and its mounting.
3. Fixed camera close-up, if supplied.
4. PTZ camera close-up, if supplied.
5. Complete two-camera setup, if applicable.
6. Wide view showing the camera overlooking residential construction.
7. Safe entrance-coverage context without exposing a blind spot.
8. Material/storage context without revealing sensitive security details.

**Next priority — operational proof**

9. Same installation at two construction stages.
10. Installer working safely, with permission to show the person.
11. Builder viewing the camera on a phone, with screen/private details hidden.
12. Two or more additional genuine Sydney installations, each identified by general suburb only.

Take a landscape context view and a portrait equipment view. Avoid digital zoom; retain the original. Website processing: descriptive short filename, accurate visible-content alt text, dimensions, compressed WebP/JPEG and suitable responsive sizes. Lazy-load below-fold photos; eager-load only the principal above-fold image when justified. Add a factual caption and nearby explanation. Do not add suburb/service keywords to every alt text.
